## **Universal Role Skills – Martial**

*For those who live and breathe the art of armed combat.*

1. **Weapon Familiarity** – *Every hilt, grip, and balance is second nature.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Gain +1 Fortune on attacks with all weapons you are proficient in.
   **Human Readable:** Your familiarity with weapons improves your accuracy.

2. **Quick Draw** – *The fight starts on your terms.*
   **Mechanics:** On Use (free). Once per round, draw or ready a weapon as part of another action.
   **Human Readable:** You can ready your weapon instantly.

3. **Combat Reflexes** – *Never caught flat-footed.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Gain +1 to Initiative rolls and +1 Fortune on Reactions.
   **Human Readable:** You react faster than your opponents expect.

4. **Versatile Striker** – *Every angle, every style.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Once per combat, switch your attack’s damage type (slashing, piercing, crushing) after rolling.
   **Human Readable:** You can adjust your weapon’s strike mid-swing.

5. **Precision Training** – *Aim for the weak points.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. All attacks ignore 1 point of armour resistance.
   **Human Readable:** You’ve learned how to bypass armour.
