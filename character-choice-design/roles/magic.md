Universal Role Skills — Magic

Arcane Focus – You channel energy with precision.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Your next magic attack gains +1 Fortune.
Human Readable: Make your next spell more likely to succeed.

Mana Flow – You adjust the rhythm of your casting.
Mechanics: On Use (free). Reduce the action cost of your next magic skill this turn by 1 (minimum 0). Usable once per round.
Human Readable: Make your next spell faster to cast.

Elemental Attunement – You weave your element into another.
Mechanics: On Use (free). Add an elemental damage type (Fire, Cold, Electric, Acid) to your next magic attack this turn.
Human Readable: Infuse your spell with an extra damage type.

Spell Ward – Your arcane shield flickers to life.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Gain +1 resistance to all magic damage types for 2 rounds.
Human Readable: Reduce damage from magical attacks for a short time.

Overchannel – You push more energy than is safe.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Your next magic attack this turn deals +3 damage but you gain 1 Pain wound.
Human Readable: Cast with more force at the cost of self-harm.
