Universal Role Skills — Acolyte

Meditative Focus – You centre your thoughts on your higher purpose.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Remove Distracted and regain 1 resource (Faith, Mana, etc. — matches your Path).
Human Readable: Take a moment to steady your mind and restore your divine energy.

Symbol of Authority – Your holy symbol commands attention.
Mechanics: On Use (free). Apply Threatened to an enemy within 3 spaces until the start of your next turn.
Human Readable: Intimidate foes into focusing on you or your message.

Sacred Sense – You attune to the presence of the profane.
Mechanics: Passive. You can detect the location (but not identity) of evil-aligned creatures within 6 spaces, even through obstacles.
Human Readable: Always sense nearby evil presences.

Scriptural Knowledge – Your knowledge of holy texts is vast.
Mechanics: Passive. +2 Fortune on checks to identify religious symbols, rites, or supernatural creatures tied to your faith.
Human Readable: You’re more likely to succeed at religious knowledge checks.

Rite of Purity – A blessing for the journey ahead.
Mechanics: On Use (2 actions). Target ally gains immunity to Poison for the next 3 rounds.
Human Readable: Protect an ally from poison effects for a short time.
