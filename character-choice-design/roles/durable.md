## **Universal Role Skills – Durable**

*You can take punishment and keep going.*

1. **Second Skin** – *Armour is part of you now.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Increase Guard by +3.
   **Human Readable:** You get more protection from your armour.

2. **Iron Breath** – *Steady, calm, unbroken.*
   **Mechanics:** On Use (free). Once per combat, remove 1 Staggered or Disoriented status from yourself.
   **Human Readable:** You shake off impairments with sheer control.

3. **Resist Pain** – *Pain is just another challenge.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Gain resistance 1 to Pain damage type.
   **Human Readable:** You’re less affected by painful strikes.

4. **Fortified Mind** – *Body and mind endure together.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Gain +1 Will and +1 Constitution.
   **Human Readable:** You’re mentally and physically tougher.

5. **Guard Recovery** – *You can always raise it again.*
   **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Restore Guard equal to half your Tier (rounded up). Usable once per round.
   **Human Readable:** You can quickly rebuild your defences mid-fight.
