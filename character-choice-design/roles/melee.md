## **Universal Role Skills – Melee**

*Close-quarters combat is your domain.*

1. **Closing Step** – *Never let them get away.*
   **Mechanics:** Reaction. When an enemy moves out of melee range, move 1 space toward them.
   **Human Readable:** You follow enemies trying to retreat.

2. **Heavy Grip** – *Strike harder by planting your weight.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. If you don’t move on your turn, your first melee attack deals +2 damage.
   **Human Readable:** Standing firm lets you hit harder.

3. **Follow-Through** – *One hit flows into the next.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. On a melee hit, gain +1 Fortune on your next melee attack this turn.
   **Human Readable:** A successful strike sets you up for the next one.

4. **Shoulder Check** – *Break their rhythm with your body.*
   **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Melee attack; on hit, apply Staggered.
   **Human Readable:** A shove or body slam disrupts their defence.

5. **Close Threat** – *Your reach is constant danger.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Enemies starting their turn adjacent to you gain 5 Risk.
   **Human Readable:** Being near you is inherently dangerous.
