Scout Role – Universal Skills

Silent Step
The forest floor doesn’t even know you passed.
Mechanics: Passive. Gain +1 Fortune on Stealth checks and movement does not break the hidden status.
Human Readable: You can move without revealing yourself and are better at staying unseen.

Eyes in the Back
You’re never truly surprised.
Mechanics: Passive. Enemies cannot gain surprise bonuses against you; +1 Wits on initiative rolls.
Human Readable: You’re always alert, spotting ambushes before they happen.

Recon Sweep
Your gaze covers ground like a hawk in flight.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Scan all enemies within line of sight; learn Guard, Risk, and any current statuses.
Human Readable: Spend 1 action to quickly gather tactical intel on everyone you can see.

Pathfinder
You know the safest way forward.
Mechanics: Passive. Reduce movement penalties from difficult terrain by half.
Human Readable: Move more easily through rough terrain, mud, snow, or rubble.

Ghost in the Canopy
They can’t hit what they can’t see.
Mechanics: On Use (2 actions). Move to any square within 5 spaces that grants cover and gain Hidden until the start of your next turn.
Human Readable: Slip into cover and vanish from view mid-combat.

Target Spotter
Point them out and watch the arrows fly.
Mechanics: On Use (free). Mark one enemy; allies gain +1 Fortune on attacks against that target until the start of your next turn.
Human Readable: Call out a target, making it easier for your allies to land hits.

Quick Hide
Gone in a blink.
Mechanics: Reaction. Trigger: You are targeted by an attack while adjacent to cover. Make a Stealth check; on success, gain Hidden before the attack resolves.
Human Readable: Duck out of sight at the last moment to foil incoming attacks.

Trail Signs
You leave messages only allies can read.
Mechanics: On Use (free). Create a temporary mark that allies can read to gain +1 Fortune on their next skill check in this area.
Human Readable: Place hidden signals to guide your allies.

Ambush Prep
You always plan the first strike.
Mechanics: Passive. When you begin combat Hidden, gain +2 Lethal on your first attack.
Human Readable: Strike deadlier when the fight starts on your terms.

Fade Away
One moment you’re there, the next you’re gone.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Remove yourself from all enemies’ observation; gain Hidden and +1 movement until the start of your next turn.
Human Readable: Break line of sight and reposition before they can track you.
