Vanguard Role – Universal Skills

Relentless Pursuit
Once you’ve marked a target, there’s no escape.
Mechanics: Passive. +2 movement when moving directly toward a marked or chosen target.
Human Readable: You close distance faster; gain +2 movement when moving toward your target.

Overrun
Push through, break their line.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Move up to 2 spaces through enemy spaces; each enemy passed gains 5 Risk.
Human Readable: Spend 1 action to surge through enemy lines, rattling those you brush past.

Break Their Guard
The first hit opens the way for the second.
Mechanics: On Hit. Target’s Guard is reduced by an additional 3.
Human Readable: Your blows smash through defences, leaving your enemy more vulnerable.

Adrenal Surge
The thrill of the fight pushes you onward.
Mechanics: On Hit. If you wound the target, gain 1 action immediately.
Human Readable: Land a wounding blow and ride the momentum into another move.

Marked for the Kill
They can run, but they can’t hide.
Mechanics: On Use (free). Choose one enemy in line of sight; all attacks you make against them gain +1 Lethal until the start of your next turn.
Human Readable: You single out prey for ruthless focus, making your finishers deadlier.

Shoulder Check
A wall of muscle in motion.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Move into target’s space; opposed Strength check. On success, target is knocked prone.
Human Readable: Spend 1 action to bodily knock someone off their feet.

Sweeping Advance
Enemies fall before the storm.
Mechanics: On Use (2 actions). Melee attack against all enemies in a 90° arc in front of you; shared roll, separate damage calculations.
Human Readable: Spend 2 actions to sweep through nearby enemies with a single, wide strike.

Unyielding Front
Stand firm, take the hit.
Mechanics: Passive. When you reduce an enemy’s Guard to 0, you gain +5 temporary Guard until the start of your next turn.
Human Readable: Dropping someone’s defences steels your own for the next blow.

Overpowering Strike
They can block the first blow… but not the second.
Mechanics: On Use (2 actions). Melee attack that cannot be parried; deals +2 damage.
Human Readable: A crushing blow that smashes through any attempt to block.

Reckless Drive
You throw caution to the wind.
Mechanics: Passive. At the start of your turn, you may choose to gain +2 damage on all attacks this round; if you do, your Guard is reduced by 5 until the end of the round.
Human Readable: Trade protection for aggression — hit harder, but leave yourself open.

## **Universal Role Skills – Vanguard**

*Strike fast, strike first, and keep the pressure on.*

1. **Forward Momentum** – *Every step is an attack.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. After moving at least 2 spaces toward an enemy, your first melee attack deals +1 damage.
   **Human Readable:** Movement fuels your offence.

2. **Opportunist’s Drive** – *Seize every weakness.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. Gain +1 Lethal when attacking Off Guard targets.
   **Human Readable:** You hit harder when they’re open.

3. **Sweeping Pressure** – *Make them defend, not attack.*
   **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Melee attack; on hit, target gains Distracted for 1 round.
   **Human Readable:** Your attack keeps them on the defensive.

4. **Risk Taker** – *The bolder the strike, the greater the result.*
   **Mechanics:** Passive. If you start your turn with Guard 10 or less, gain +2 damage on your first attack.
   **Human Readable:** You hit hardest when you’re most exposed.

5. **Pushing Assault** – *Drive them back and keep going.*
   **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Melee attack; on hit, push target 1 space and gain +1 movement.
   **Human Readable:** You hit while advancing your position.
