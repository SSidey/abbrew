Ranged Role – Universal Skills

Eagle-Eyed
Far is near.
Mechanics: Passive. Ignore the first range penalty tier and double effective long-range distance for penalty purposes.
Human Readable: You see further than most; ignore the first range penalty and treat long range as twice as far before penalties apply.

Suppressive Fire
Heads down.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Successful hit causes target to lose 1 action next turn.
Human Readable: Spend 1 action to fire in a way that forces your target to duck or freeze, costing them 1 action on their next turn.

Pinning Shot
Pinned like a moth.
Mechanics: On Hit. Target’s movement is halved until end of its next turn.
Human Readable: You nail your target in place, slowing their movement to half speed.

Trick Shot
Angles are allies.
Mechanics: On Use, 1/encounter. Next attack ignores cover.
Human Readable: Once per encounter, you can bend a shot around obstacles, ignoring cover penalties.

Volley Fire
Many fall to many arrows.
Mechanics: On Use (2 actions). Attack up to 3 adjacent enemies within range; separate rolls.
Human Readable: Spend 2 actions to rain shots across a clustered group of up to three foes.

Longarm
Draw and loose.
Mechanics: Passive. Can draw and fire a ranged weapon in the same action.
Human Readable: You can ready and fire your weapon in a single fluid motion without extra time.

Piercing Point
Through the rib.
Mechanics: On Hit. Adds piercing damage. If target’s Risk ≥ 20, also apply 1 Vital wound.
Human Readable: A deadly shot aimed for vital organs; at high Risk, it inflicts grievous injury.

Burning Arrow
Feathered flame.
Mechanics: On Hit. Adds fire damage and applies Burning (2 lingering).
Human Readable: Your shot carries flame, setting the target alight for lingering burn damage.

Armour Bypass
Slip the plates.
Mechanics: On Hit. Ignores 1 point of resistance of the damage type dealt.
Human Readable: You exploit armour gaps, bypassing a small portion of their protection.

Frostbite Arrow
Slow the blood.
Mechanics: On Hit. Adds cold damage. Halves target’s movement for 2 rounds.
Human Readable: A freezing shot that slows your opponent’s every step for the next two turns.

Universal Role Skills — Ranged

Steady Aim – You align your sights with care.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Your next ranged attack this turn gains +1 Lethal.
Human Readable: Take time to make a more deadly ranged shot.

Quick Draw – Your weapon is ready before others can blink.
Mechanics: Passive. Gain +2 Initiative when wielding a ranged weapon at the start of combat.
Human Readable: You’re quicker to act when armed with ranged gear.

Pinning Shot – A well-placed hit halts movement.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Ranged attack; on a hit, target gains Immobilised for 1 round.
Human Readable: Stop an enemy in their tracks with a precise shot.

Covering Fire – You keep heads down so allies can move.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Choose an area within 4 spaces; enemies moving through it before your next turn gain +1 Risk.
Human Readable: Make an area dangerous to cross, helping allies advance.

Volley – Several shots at once blanket an area.
Mechanics: On Use (2 actions). Make one ranged attack roll and apply it to all targets in a 3x3 zone within range; deal half damage to each.
Human Readable: Fire into an area to hit multiple targets at once.
