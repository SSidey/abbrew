Universal Role Skills — Healer

Field Dressing – Quick patchwork to keep them going.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Remove 1 acute wound from an adjacent ally.
Human Readable: Treat a minor injury quickly.

Soothing Presence – Your calm steadies the wounded.
Mechanics: Passive. Allies within 2 spaces gain +1 Fortune on rolls to recover from lingering wounds.
Human Readable: Make it easier for nearby allies to heal over time.

Pain Easing Touch – A gentle touch dulls agony.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Remove 1 Pain wound from a target within 1 space.
Human Readable: Relieve pain from an ally.

Battlefield Medic – You don’t stop moving while you heal.
Mechanics: Passive. You may move 1 space before or after using a healing skill without spending additional actions.
Human Readable: Heal while repositioning.

Shared Vitality – You give of yourself to aid another.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action). Transfer 1 acute wound from an adjacent ally to yourself.
Human Readable: Take on an ally’s injury as your own.
