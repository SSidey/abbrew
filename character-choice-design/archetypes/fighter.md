Archetype: Warrior

Theme:
A master at arms, utilising weapons, their physical prowess, and their mind for combat to its fullest. Warriors adapt to any battlefield, pairing skill with endurance and cunning tactics. Whether armed with sword, axe, spear, or shield, their focus is on perfecting martial technique and dominating their chosen style.

Requirements:

    Per Rank: 4 skills from any martial role source (Path or Universal Role Skills).

    Remaining skill for each rank can come from chosen Path, Universal Role Skills, or Profession synergy.

Restrictions:

    Cannot take skills with ranged or magic roles.

Profession Synergies:

    Blacksmith (crafting and reinforcing weapons/armour)

    Soldier (drilled formations, battlefield tactics)

    Guard (protection and control of space)

Warrior Archetype Tier Skills

Rank 1 Tier Skill – Weapon Mastery
Every weapon is an extension of yourself.
Mechanics: Passive. Gain +1 Fortune on all attacks with weapons you are proficient in; you count as proficient with all martial melee weapons.
Human Readable: You’re equally deadly with any melee weapon, and your attacks are more accurate.

Rank 6 Tier Skill – Battle Rhythm (Specialisation Skill)
You know the flow of combat, and bend it to your will.
Mechanics: Passive. Whenever you successfully hit with a melee attack, gain 1 extra action (max once per turn). Additionally, your melee attacks deal +1 damage if you did not move this turn.
Human Readable: Each successful strike keeps your momentum going; hitting makes you faster, standing your ground makes you hit harder.

Rank 10 Tier Skill – Master of Arms (Master Skill)
There is no stance, style, or trick you have not mastered.
Mechanics: Passive. All melee attacks gain +1 Lethal and ignore 1 point of resistance. You may ready or swap weapons as a free action once per turn.
Human Readable: Your mastery makes you unstoppable — your blows cut through defences, and you can switch weapons without losing tempo.
