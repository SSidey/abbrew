Cleric Archetype Tier Skills

Rank 1 Tier Skill – Sacred Channel
The divine flows through you, ready to be unleashed.
Mechanics: Passive. You gain the Faith resource (max 5). You can spend 1 Faith to add +2 Fortune to any healing or smiting skill. Regain 1 Faith at the start of your turn if you did not attack that turn.
Human Readable: You can store divine energy and release it to heal or smite more effectively.

Rank 6 Tier Skill – Anointed Champion (Specialisation Skill)
Your god’s blessing strengthens both your body and your will.
Mechanics: Passive. Gain +1 Will and +1 to your highest physical stat. Once per combat, spend 2 Faith to gain immunity to Staggered, Disoriented, and Off Guard for 1 round.
Human Readable: You become physically and mentally fortified by divine blessing, able to shrug off debilitating effects.

Rank 10 Tier Skill – Avatar of the Divine (Master Skill)
The full might of your god manifests through you.
Mechanics: On Use (free). Once per day, for 3 rounds you gain +2 damage to all attacks, healing you perform restores +2 HP, and you are immune to Death, Pain, and Fear damage types. While active, you radiate light in a 3-space radius that dispels magical darkness. Costs 3 Faith to activate.
Human Readable: For a short time, you embody your deity’s power, striking harder, healing more, and banishing darkness and evil.
