## **Path: Shielded Fighter**

**Theme:**
*A shielded fighter blends offence and defence, using weapon and shield in perfect balance. They hold the line, protect allies, and create openings with controlled aggression. In their hands, a shield is both a wall and a weapon — the perfect partner to a keen blade.*

**Roles:**

* `martial`
* `melee`
* `durable`
* `vanguard`

---

### **Path Tier Skills**

**Rank 1 Tier Skill – Wall and Edge**
*Every strike is guarded, every guard is a threat.*
**Mechanics:** Passive. Gain +2 Guard when you hit with a melee attack while wielding a shield. When you successfully block an attack with a shield, your next melee attack deals +1 damage.
**Human Readable:** Fighting with a shield strengthens both your defence and offence — every hit protects you, every block makes your counterattack stronger.

---

**Rank 6 Tier Skill – Bulwark’s Advance** *(Specialisation Skill)*
*Your shield leads the way, unyielding and unstoppable.*
**Mechanics:** On Use (2 actions). Move up to your speed in a straight line, pushing enemies 1 space aside and gaining +5 temporary Guard until the start of your next turn.
**Human Readable:** You march forward behind your shield, forcing enemies back and bolstering your defences.

---

**Rank 10 Tier Skill – Impregnable Bastion** *(Master Skill)*
*Nothing passes without your leave.*
**Mechanics:** Passive. Gain immunity to being pushed or knocked prone while wielding a shield. Allies adjacent to you gain +2 Guard. Once per round, when an adjacent ally is attacked, you may redirect the attack to yourself.
**Human Readable:** You become a living fortress, protecting allies and standing your ground no matter the assault.
