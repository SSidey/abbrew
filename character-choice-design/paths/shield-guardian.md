# **Path: Shield Guardian**

**Theme:**
*A Shield Guardian fights not for glory, but for others. They dedicate themselves to defence, wielding their shield as both weapon and bastion. They interpose between allies and danger, using tactics, grit, and sheer will to keep their companions safe. Their fighting style is defined by zoning, protective auras, and drawing enemy focus. The essence of a Shield Guardian is sacrifice — taking blows meant for others, and thriving in the front line where risk is greatest.*

**Roles:** `martial`, `melee`, `protector`, `durable`

---

### **Core Mechanics**

* **Resource – Duty**
  *Represents the Guardian’s resolve to protect others.*

  * Maximum: **5 Duty**
  * Gained from protective actions (blocking, defending a charge, standing ground).
  * Spent on special Path skills (fortifications, interventions).

* **Initiate Skill – Duty**
  *You commit yourself to the protection of others.*

  * **Mechanics:** On Use (free). Gain 1 Duty whenever you:

    * Start your turn adjacent to your designated charge.
    * Successfully block or reduce damage with your shield.
    * Redirect an attack to yourself.

* **Initiate Skill – Designate Charge**
  *You choose one ally as your sworn responsibility.*

  * **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Select one ally in sight as your *Charge* for this combat.
  * They gain +10 maximum Guard.
  * Certain Shield Guardian skills gain extra effects when used to protect your Charge.
  * You may only have one Charge at a time; you can reassign with another use of this skill.

---

### **Tier Skills**

**Rank 1 Tier Skill – Stand Firm** *(cost: 1 Tier Die)*
*You are the wall between allies and harm.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Duty, 1 Tier Die). Until the start of your next turn, any time your Charge is attacked and within 2 spaces of you, you may redirect the attack to yourself.
* **Human Readable:** You can spend a die and Duty to force enemies to strike you instead of your protected ally.

---

**Rank 6 Tier Skill – Shieldwall Champion** *(cost: 2 Tier Dice)*
*Your defence strengthens everyone around you.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. While adjacent to your Charge, both of you gain +2 Guard. Spend 2 Tier Dice (free action) to extend this to all allies within 2 spaces until the start of your next turn.
* **Human Readable:** You and your Charge are always tougher together, and you can spend dice to extend that protection to nearby allies.

---

**Rank 10 Tier Skill – Aegis Eternal** *(cost: 3 Tier Dice)*
*Your shield becomes a bastion none can bypass.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 Duty, 3 Tier Dice). For 1 round, your Charge cannot be targeted by attacks unless you are reduced to 0 Guard first. During this time, you gain +2 resistance to all physical and magical damage.
* **Human Readable:** For one round, you make it nearly impossible for enemies to strike your Charge — they must go through you.
