# **Path: Divine Adherent**

**Theme:**
*Those who walk the path of a Divine Adherent are marked by devotion to a god or pantheon. Their power does not come from within, but is granted by divine grace in exchange for faith and service. Through prayer they gather **Faith**, which they spend to heal the wounded, bless allies, consecrate ground, or smite their foes with miraculous wrath. They are shepherds, guardians, and sometimes avengers, depending on the god they serve.*

**Roles:** `acolyte`, `ranged`, `magic`, `healer`

---

## **Core Mechanic – Faith**

* **Faith (Resource):**

  * Maximum: **10 Faith**
  * Start each day at **0 Faith**
  * Restores to **0 at dawn** (each day begins fresh).
  * Spent on **Blessings** (buffs, consecrations, protections) and **Miracles** (direct divine interventions).

* **Initiate Skill – Pray**
  *You offer a moment of devotion to your god.*

  * **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Gain 1 Faith. If you are adjacent to your deity’s holy symbol, altar, or sanctified ground, gain 2 Faith instead.
  * **Human Readable:** Spend an action to pray, building up Faith for future powers.

* **Initiate Skill – Follower of \[Deity]**
  *Your faith ties you to a higher power.*

  * **Mechanics:** Passive. Choose one deity (or divine ideal). Grants Pray, the Faith resource, and 1 special Blessing unique to that deity.
  * **Human Readable:** Your devotion grants you the ability to generate Faith, and a unique divine gift depending on your god.
  * **Example Deity Blessings:**

    * **God of War:** On Use (1 Faith). An ally’s next melee attack deals +2 damage.
    * **Goddess of Mercy:** On Use (1 Faith). Remove 1 Pain or Fatigue wound from an ally.
    * **God of Knowledge:** On Use (1 Faith). Ally gains +1 Fortune on their next check or attack.
    * **God of Death:** On Use (1 Faith). Next spell you cast also applies 1 Necrotic wound.

## **Tier Skills**

---

### **Rank 1 Tier Skill – Channel Blessing** *(cost: 1 Tier Die)*

*The divine listens to your plea.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 Tier Die). Choose one:

  * Ally within 3 spaces gains +2 Guard.
  * Enemy within 3 spaces gains +5 Risk.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith and a die to either shield an ally or weaken a foe.

---

### **Rank 6 Tier Skill – Miracle of Light** *(cost: 2 Tier Dice)*

*The heavens answer with brilliance.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 Faith, 2 Tier Dice, 2 actions). Create a 3x3 zone of radiant light for 2 rounds.

  * Allies inside: heal 1 wound at the start of their turn.
  * Enemies inside: take 1 Light damage at the start of their turn.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith and dice to sanctify an area, healing allies and burning foes within it.

---

### **Rank 10 Tier Skill – Divine Judgment** *(cost: 3 Tier Dice)*

*You call down your god’s full wrath upon the wicked.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (3 Faith, 3 Tier Dice, 2 actions). Choose 1 enemy within 8 spaces. They take 4 Light damage, 2 Vital wounds, and must pass a Will check or be Staggered.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith and dice to smite one foe with overwhelming divine power.

* **Pray** + **Follower of X** as their initiate package.
* Tier Skills scaling from simple divine favour (buffs/debuffs) → miracles (zones of blessing/smite) → judgment (god-tier single target execution).

Perfect clarification 🙌 — thank you. Yes, the **Divine Adherent** is the **broad “faith path”** that underpins a spectrum of archetypes:

* **Priest** → fully Adherent, miracles & blessings.
* **Cleric** → mostly Adherent, but blended with martial skill.
* **Paladin** → half Adherent, half martial defender.
* **Warpriest** → mostly martial, with just a touch of divine channeling.

So the Path itself should be **flexible**, offering:

* **Blessings** (buffs & protections),
* **Miracles** (flashy divine effects, smites, consecrations),
* **Healing & restoration**,
* And some **utility** like sanctifying ground, bolstering morale, or invoking divine guidance.

Let’s build **Ranks 1–3** with that in mind: a mixture of **Faith generators, small Blessings, direct Miracles, and light healing**, without going full “cleric kit.”

# **Divine Adherent Path Skills (Ranks 1–3)**

---

## **Rank 1 Skills**

1. **Duty of Care** *(Blessing)*
   *You offer divine comfort to your chosen.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 action). Remove 1 Pain or Fatigue wound from an ally within 2 spaces.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to ease an ally’s suffering.

---

2. **Consecrate Weapon** *(Miracle)*
   *Your god guides your ally’s strike.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 action). Target ally’s next melee or ranged attack gains +1 Lethal and counts as Light damage in addition to its normal type.
* **Human Readable:** Bless an ally’s attack to pierce harder and shine with divine light.

---

3. **Guiding Prayer** *(Blessing)*
   *You whisper words of direction and hope.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, free). Target ally gains +1 Fortune on their next roll before the end of your next turn.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to give an ally divine guidance.

---

4. **Sacred Vigil** *(Generator)*
   *You remain alert in your god’s presence.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. Gain 1 Faith whenever you begin your turn within 2 spaces of a holy symbol, altar, or consecrated ground.
* **Human Readable:** Being near sacred objects strengthens your Faith.

---

5. **Hand of Penance** *(Miracle)*
   *You channel righteous punishment.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 action). Make a ranged spell attack against a target within 6 spaces; deal 2 Light damage.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to smite an enemy with holy power.

---

## **Rank 2 Skills**

1. **Sanctified Ground** *(Blessing)*
   *Your god claims this place for a moment.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 2 actions). Create a 2-space radius zone lasting 2 rounds. Allies inside gain +1 Guard.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to consecrate a small area that protects allies.

---

2. **Mercy’s Touch** *(Blessing/Healing)*
   *Gentle words mend what steel cannot.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 action). Remove 1 acute wound of your choice from an adjacent ally.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to heal an ally’s wound.

---

3. **Eyes of the Faithful** *(Utility)*
   *Your god grants you a glimpse of truth.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, free). For the next round, you can see Hidden enemies within 6 spaces as if they were Off Guard.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to reveal hidden foes.

---

4. **Smite of Wrath** *(Miracle)*
   *The heavens strike through your hand.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 Faith, 2 actions). Make a melee or ranged spell attack; deal 3 Light damage and apply 1 Dread wound.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to unleash a divine strike that terrifies the target.

---

5. **Prayer of Fortitude** *(Blessing)*
   *Your words steel your allies’ hearts.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 action). All allies within 2 spaces gain +1 Will until the start of your next turn.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to boost allies’ mental resilience.

---

## **Rank 3 Skills**

1. **Miracle of Restoration** *(Healing)*
   *Your god restores flesh as easily as clay.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 Faith, 2 actions). Remove 2 wounds of your choice from an adjacent ally.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to heal significant wounds.

---

2. **Sacred Interdiction** *(Miracle)*
   *You forbid the profane to draw near.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 action). Choose 1 enemy within 4 spaces; they must succeed a Will check or be Immobilised until the start of your next turn.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to halt an enemy’s advance with divine command.

---

3. **Light in Darkness** *(Blessing)*
   *Your god’s light drives away the shadows.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 Faith, 1 action). Illuminate a 3-space radius around you for 2 rounds. Enemies in the light cannot be Hidden.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to light an area and reveal concealed foes.

---

4. **Retributive Flame** *(Miracle)*
   *Your god punishes those who strike your chosen.*

* **Mechanics:** Reaction (1 Faith). When your Charge or ally within 3 spaces is hit, deal 2 Fire + Light damage back to the attacker.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to smite those who wound your allies.

---

5. **Hymn of Endurance** *(Blessing)*
   *You bolster allies with song and scripture.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 Faith, 2 actions). All allies within 3 spaces gain +2 Guard until the start of your next turn.
* **Human Readable:** Spend Faith to reinforce nearby allies’ defences.

---

✨ That gives you:

* **Rank 1:** starter tools — 2 Blessings, 1 Miracle, 1 Generator, 1 heal-lite.
* **Rank 2:** expansion into healing, smiting, zones, morale buffs.
* **Rank 3:** “proper miracles” — big heals, interdictions, divine light, retaliation, and group endurance.

They’re broad enough to work for a **Priest** or **Paladin hybrid**, without being locked into “just a healer.”
