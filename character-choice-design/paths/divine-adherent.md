## **Path: Divine Adherent**

**Theme:**
*You worship a deity and draw power through prayer, devotion, and the rites of your faith. Your power is measured in Faith, a sacred resource gained through moments of devotion or acts aligned with your god’s will. You can channel this energy to heal, smite, protect, or bolster allies, weaving divine magic into every battlefield action.*

**Primary Roles:** `acolyte`, `ranged`, `magic`, `healer`

**Core Mechanic:** **Faith** — You begin combat with 2 Faith (max 5). You regain 1 Faith at the start of your turn if you use no offensive abilities that turn, or whenever you complete a prayer action (usually 1 action, sometimes free via certain skills). Faith powers many of your most potent effects.
