Theme:
The Barbarian is a whirlwind of fury and raw strength. They throw themselves into combat heedless of defence, trusting rage to carry them through wounds that would fell lesser fighters. Where others parry, they press forward; where others falter, they roar defiance. Their fury makes them terrifying to face — but also exposes them to danger if their rage burns too long.

Rage (Core Ability)

Unleash your fury in battle.
Mechanics: On Use (1 action, once per combat). Enter Rage for the entire combat. While Raging:

+2 damage on all melee attacks.

Immune to Rage wounds.

Take +5 Risk whenever attacked.

Cannot use Feint, Parry, or Dodge.
Human Readable: When you rage, you become more dangerous and reckless, trading defence for sheer aggression.

Tier Skills

Rank 1 Tier Skill – Bloodrush (cost: 1 Tier Die)
Each wound fuels the storm.
Mechanics: Passive. While Raging, whenever you take a wound, immediately gain +1 Fortune on your next melee attack.
Human Readable: Getting hurt only makes your next strike more likely to land.

Rank 6 Tier Skill – Furious Momentum (cost: 2 Tier Dice)
The harder you hit, the harder you press.
Mechanics: On Use. While Raging, when you deal a wound, spend 2 Tier Dice to immediately make a bonus melee attack (costs no actions, once per round).
Human Readable: Spend dice to keep swinging after landing a serious hit.

Rank 10 Tier Skill – Avatar of Wrath (cost: 3 Tier Dice)
You are the battlefield made fury.
Mechanics: On Use. For the next 2 rounds while Raging: +4 damage on all melee attacks, +2 Lethal, immunity to Fear wounds, ignore 1 resistance. Costs 3 Tier Dice.
Human Readable: Spend dice to enter a peak state of divine wrath, overwhelming all foes with brutal strikes.

# **Barbarian Path Skills**

---

## **Rank 1 Skills**

1. **Raging Blow** *(Rage-only)*
   *Your fury turns a strike into a crushing smash.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Melee attack; while Raging, deal +3 damage and apply 1 Fatigue wound.
* **Human Readable:** A devastating swing that tires the target — but only possible when raging.

---

2. **Savage Charge** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *You hurl yourself into battle heedless of danger.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 actions). Move up to 4 spaces in a straight line and make a melee attack at the end. If Raging, push the target 2 spaces and apply Staggered.
* **Human Readable:** Charge forward to strike; if raging, you slam into them hard enough to knock them reeling.

---

3. **Frenzied Strikes** *(Rage-only)*
   *In the storm of fury, one blow is never enough.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 actions). Make 2 melee attacks against the same target. Each deals –2 damage, but if both hit, apply 1 Bleed wound.
* **Human Readable:** Trade precision for sheer volume of strikes, causing bleeding if both land.

---

4. **Battle Roar** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *Your fury shakes even hardened foes.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (free). All enemies within 3 spaces must pass a Will check or become Distracted for 1 round. If Raging, also apply Off Guard to one target of your choice.
* **Human Readable:** A terrifying shout that rattles enemies; even more disorienting when raging.

---

5. **Thick-Skulled** *(Neutral)*
   *Blows that would stagger others only feed your anger.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. The first time you would gain Staggered each combat, ignore it. If Raging, gain +1 Fortune on your next attack instead.
* **Human Readable:** You shrug off the first staggering hit, and when raging, you turn it into momentum.

---

## **Rank 2 Skills**

1. **Blood for Blood** *(Rage-only)*
   *Every cut you take is repaid in kind.*

* **Mechanics:** Reaction. When you suffer a wound while Raging, make a melee attack for 0 actions against the attacker. Usable once per round.
* **Human Readable:** When you’re wounded, you immediately lash back in fury.

---

2. **Savage Grapple** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *You wrestle them down with brute force.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Make a Strength check against a target in melee; on success, apply Grabbed. If Raging, also apply Prone.
* **Human Readable:** Drag an enemy into your grip; if raging, you smash them down into the dirt.

---

3. **Wound-Drunk** *(Rage-only)*
   *Pain becomes fuel for fury.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. While Raging, whenever you gain a wound, gain +1 damage on all attacks until the end of your next turn.
* **Human Readable:** Each wound you take makes your next blows hit harder.

---

4. **Reckless Swing** *(Neutral)*
   *Accuracy is for cowards — swing until something breaks.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 actions). Make a melee attack with +3 damage and –1 Fortune. If Raging, add +1 Lethal.
* **Human Readable:** A risky but punishing strike, made even more lethal when raging.

---

5. **Howl of Defiance** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *Your voice rises above the clash of steel.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Allies within 2 spaces gain +1 Will until the start of your next turn. If Raging, also restore +2 Guard to each ally affected.
* **Human Readable:** Bolster your allies’ resolve, and when raging, shield them with your fury.

---

## **Rank 3 Skills**

1. **Decapitating Strike** *(Rage-only, Finisher)*
   *Your fury finds the weak point.*

* **Mechanics:** Finisher. Spend 20 Risk. Deal +3 damage and apply 2 Slashing wounds. If Raging, increase to 3 Slashing wounds.
* **Human Readable:** A savage execution strike that cleaves flesh — deadlier while raging.

---

2. **Berserker’s Momentum** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *Nothing halts your advance.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. Whenever you drop an enemy to Defeated or Dead, move 2 spaces. If Raging, also gain +1 Fortune on your next attack this turn.
* **Human Readable:** Your fury drives you forward after every kill.

---

3. **Savage Headbutt** *(Neutral)*
   *Steel meets skull, and skull wins.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (1 action). Melee attack dealing 2 Crushing damage; on hit, apply Staggered. If Raging, deal +1 Pain wound.
* **Human Readable:** A brutal, reckless headbutt that staggers or injures the target.

---

4. **Endless Fury** *(Rage-only)*
   *Your rage is a shield as well as a weapon.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. While Raging, whenever you inflict a wound, restore 2 Guard (max once per turn).
* **Human Readable:** Each wound you deal while raging toughens you for the next blow.

---

5. **Bloodcurdling Cry** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *Your roar tears through courage like steel through flesh.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 actions). All enemies within 3 spaces must pass a Will check or gain Dread 1. If Raging, also apply Terror 1 to one chosen target.
* **Human Readable:** You terrify enemies nearby, with one victim utterly shaken when you’re raging.

# **Barbarian Path Skills (Ranks 7–10)**

1. **Skull Crusher** *(Rage-only, Finisher)*
   *One blow to end the fight.*

* **Human Readable:** A brutal head-cracking strike that can instantly end a foe.

---

2. **Crimson Resilience** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *Blood loss only steels your resolve.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. While you have Bleed lingering wounds, gain +1 Constitution. If Raging, also reduce Bleed loss by 1 each round.
* **Human Readable:** Bleeding doesn’t slow you — it hardens you.

---

3. **Berserker’s Leap** *(Neutral)*
   *Leap upon them like a predator.*

* **Mechanics:** On Use (2 actions). Jump up to 3 spaces and make a melee attack on landing; deal +2 damage if you moved at least 2 spaces. If Raging, also apply Staggered.
* **Human Readable:** Slam into enemies with your whole body, staggering them in rage.

---

4. **Savage Counter** *(Augmented by Rage)*
   *Their attack opens them up to yours.*

* **Mechanics:** Reaction. When hit by a melee attack, deal 2 damage back. If Raging, also apply +5 Risk to the attacker.
* **Human Readable:** Even when struck, you make sure they regret it.

---

5. **Eternal Fury** *(Neutral)*
   *Your rage refuses to die.*

* **Mechanics:** Passive. When reduced to 0 Guard, stay conscious for 1 extra round before being Defeated. If Raging, gain +1 attack that round.
* **Human Readable:** You fight on past your body’s limits, swinging even after collapse.
