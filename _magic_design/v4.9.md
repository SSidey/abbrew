# ⚝ Codex Canonica Magicarum — v4.9

*“Through **Aether** we shape; by **Gravitas** we hold.”*

---

## Prologue

In every working two forces meet. **Aether** is the mind’s ascent—what the caster can bend by insight and focus. **Gravitas** is the world’s weight—what must be anchored with scope and time.

This edition replaces “active/passive” with:

* **Aetheric Entropy** (🜂): **Intent**, **Concept**, **Distance** — malleable by cognition.
* **Gravitic Entropy** (🜃): **Area**, **Duration** — resistant; the world demands a price.

A third sign, **Equilibria** (⚖), is used as flavour: the ideal balance of the two.

---

# I · The Core Grammar of a Spell

A spell phrase is:
**Intent** + **Concept** + **Distance** (+ **Area**) (+ **Duration**)

* Sum **Aetheric Entropy** (Intent + Concept + Distance) → your **power** cost.
* Sum **Gravitic Entropy** (Area + Duration) → your **time** cost (ritual/stability).

You then apply reductions:

**Knowledge & Focus → Entropy ↓ 0 → Actions**

* **Intelligence** reduces **studied** Aetheric components (Intent/Concept/Distance) from e → **0e**, replacing each with its base **action** cost.
* **Visualisation** applied through **foci** reduces Concept (and, where applicable, Distance) Entropy by 1e per Vis.

**Materia (Verbum & Gestus) → Actions ↓**

* **Verbum** (spoken) and **Gestus** (somatic) do **not** reduce Entropy; they reduce total **actions** by **–1a** each (–2a if both), representing mnemonic efficiency.

**Resolve & Wealth → Time ↓**

* **Gravitic Entropy** maps to a *ritual time* band. You can:

  * Spend **Resolve** (1 Resolve = 1e of Gravitic) to compress time, or
  * Invest materials (gp) if a focus/anchor is part of the casting or item-making rules.

## II · Aetheric Entropy (🜂): Intent, Concept, Distance

### 1) Intent (Ars Voluntas)

* **Creare** (Create) … **1e**
* **Destruere** (Destroy) … **1e** *(see v4.6 rule for matching duration if unmaking an ongoing effect)*
* **Formare** (Shape) … **2e**
* **Movere / Sistere** (Move/Stop) … **1e**
* **Infundere / Extrahere** (Infuse/Extract) … **2e**
* **Protegere** (Protect from/with) … **2e**
* **Revelare / Obfuscare** (Reveal/Obscure) … **2e**
* **Temporalis** (Delay/Sustain/Revert) … **3e**
* **Transmutare** (Transform) … |ΔConcept| + **1e** (same-Circulus baseline), or use your prior distance rule if preferred.

**When reduced to 0e:** **Intent costs 1 action**.

**When reduced to 0e:** by table convention for play speed, **Concept costs 1 action** (regardless of Orbis), matching your own worked examples.

**Foci with Visualisation**

* **Simple** focus: reduce Concept Entropy by **Vis** (1 Vis = 1e).
* **Aligned/Perfected** (with training): larger caps per prior editions; still gated by Vis throughput.
* Enhancements: +300 gp = +1e capacity (to the focus’ relevant concept).

*(Distance can also be linked to spatial foci/loci if you allow it at your table; default is Concept only.)*

## III · Gravitic Entropy (🜃): Area, Duration

### 4) Area (Ars Spatium · Figura)

Add to **Gravitic Entropy**.

| Form  | Extra e | Scale per e   |
| ----- | ------: | ------------- |
| Point |     +0e | single target |
| Line  |     +1e | 10 m × 1 m    |
| Cone  |     +1e | 5 m radius    |
| Burst |     +1e | 2 m radius    |
| Wall  |     +1e | 10 m × 2 m    |

*(Increasing size beyond base can step Area e upward again, per your table if you later add tiers.)*

---

### 5) Duration (Ars Tempus)

**Important:** “Time Required” is **one band lower** than Duration.

|  e | **Time Required (Ritual)** | **Duration (Effect)** | Tier Stable |
| -: | -------------------------- | --------------------- | :---------: |
|  0 | Instant                    | Instant               |      1      |
|  1 | **1 Action**               | 1 Round               |      1      |
|  2 | **1 Round**                | 1 Minute              |      2      |
|  3 | **1 Minute**               | 10 Minutes            |      2      |
|  4 | **10 Minutes**             | 1 Hour                |      3      |
|  5 | **1 Hour**                 | 12 Hours              |      3      |
|  6 | **12 Hours**               | 1 Day                 |      4      |
|  7 | **1 Day**                  | 1 Week                |      5      |
|  8 | **1 Week**                 | 1 Month               |      6      |
|  9 | **1 Month**                | 1 Year                |      7      |
| 10 | **1 Year**                 | 10 Years              |      8      |
| 12 | **10 Years**               | Permanent             |      9      |

* If **Gravitic e ≤ Tier**, you can cast without a ritual (pure actions).
* You may spend **Resolve** (1 Resolve = 1e) to reduce **Gravitic e** and shorten the required ritual band.

## IV · Reductions & Resources (How Costs Come Down)

* **Intelligence**: for each **studied** Aetheric component, reduce its Entropy to **0e** (not below), then pay its **base actions** (Intent 1a, Concept 1a, Distance = band a).
* **Visualisation** + **Focus**: reduce Concept (and optionally Distance if allowed) Entropy by 1e per Vis through an appropriate focus (capped by focus grade and training).
* **Materia — Verbum & Gestus**: after converting Entropy → actions, reduce **total actions** by **–1a** each (–2a for both).
* **Resolve**: reduce Gravitic Entropy by 1e per Resolve, shortening the required ritual band.
* **Wealth / Craft**: upgrade foci (300 gp = +1e capacity) or use anchors/loci per earlier chapters.

> **Minimum action floor:** Any component reduced to 0e still demands **its base actions**; a spell cannot be “free.”

# V · Procedure (Checklist)

1. Write the phrase: **Intent + Concept + Distance (+Area) (+Duration)**
2. Sum **Aetheric e** (Intent + Concept + Distance).
3. Sum **Gravitic e** (Area + Duration).
4. Apply **Intelligence** study → convert selected Aetheric components to **0e → actions**.
5. Apply **Visualisation via Focus** → reduce Concept/Distance **e** further (to 0e if possible).
6. Apply **Verbum/Gestus** → reduce **actions** by –1/–2 total.
7. Check **Gravitic e** vs **Tier** and **Time Required**; spend **Resolve** as desired.
8. Cast: pay **actions** now; fulfil ritual/time if any; effect persists for **Duration**.

# VI · Worked Examples (as requested)

> **Assumptions for all three:**
> Fire = **Ignis Medius (5e)**; Near = **Band 3 (3e)**; Burst adds **+1e** Area.
> Caster has the studies (Intent: Creare; Concept: Fire; Distance: Near) and a **simple fire focus** (lit torch).
> **Verbum + Gestus** available (–2 actions total).

## 1) **Creare Ignem Proximum**

*Create Fire Near (no area, no duration)*

**Base Entropy**

* Intent: Creare **1e**
* Concept: Fire **5e**
* Distance: Near **3e**
* Area: +0e
* Duration: +0e
  **Totals:** Aetheric **9e**, Gravitic **0e**

**Reductions**

* INT studies → Intent 0e → **1a**; Concept 0e → **1a**; Distance 0e → **3a**
* Focus (Vis applied): not needed (already 0e), but could have helped if INT were lower
* Verbum + Gestus: **–2a**

**Result**

* **Actions:** 1a (Intent) + 1a (Concept) + 3a (Distance) – 2a = **3 actions**
* **Gravitic e:** 0 → **Instant**
  **Final:** **3 actions**, instantaneous effect (Tier 1 ready)

*(Matches your earlier “5a then –2a = 3a” cadence.)*

---

## 2) **Creare Ignem Proximum (Burst)**

*Add a small area; still instantaneous*

**Base Entropy**

* Aetheric: as above **9e**
* **Area:** +1e (Burst)
* **Duration:** +0e
  **Totals:** Aetheric **9e**, Gravitic **1e**

**Reductions**

* INT studies → 0e → actions = **1a + 1a + 3a**
* Verbum + Gestus → **–2a**

**Time**

* Gravitic **1e** → **Time Required = 1 Action** (from table) → a short stabilising moment

  * (You may spend **1 Resolve** to compress to **0e** for true instant release.)

**Result**

* **Casting actions:** (1 + 1 + 3 – 2) = **3 actions**
* **Time Required:** **1 action** ritual window (or pay 1 Resolve to ignore)
  **Final:** **3 actions + 1-action stabilise**, instantaneous effect; or **3 actions + 1 Resolve** for fully instant

---

## 3) **Creare Ignem Proximum (Burst, 1 Minute)**

*Add Area and a short Duration*

**Base Entropy**

* Aetheric: **9e** (as above)
* Gravitic: **Area +1e** + **Duration +2e** (1 Minute) = **3e**

**Reductions**

* INT studies → **1a + 1a + 3a**
* Verbum + Gestus → **–2a → net 3 actions**

**Time**

* Gravitic **3e** → **Time Required = 1 Minute** (one band shorter than 10-minute Duration band)
* Options to avoid waiting: spend **3 Resolve** → 0e (instant cast)

**Result**

* **Casting actions:** **3 actions**
* **Ritual time:** **1 minute**, or spend **3 Resolve** to compress to instant
  **Final:** **3 actions + 1-minute stabilise**, lasts **1 minute**; or **3 actions + 3 Resolve** for instant bring-up

# VII · Quick Reference

**Aetheric (🜂)**: Intent (1e) · Concept (per table) · Distance (band e)
**When 0e:** Intent **1a** · Concept **1a** · Distance **band a**

**Gravitic (🜃)**: Area (+1e) · Duration (per table) → **Time Required** = **one band lower** than Duration.

**Reductions**

* **Intelligence** (studied Aetheric components): e → 0e, then pay base actions
* **Visualisation via Focus**: reduce Concept (and optionally Distance) e by Vis, capped by focus grade/training
* **Verbum + Gestus**: –1a each (–2a max)
* **Resolve**: 1 Resolve = –1e Gravitic (shorten ritual)

**Design Guardrails**

* Minimum 1 action per component when reduced to 0e.
* **Destruere** must match target’s **Duration e** when unmaking ongoing effects (v4.6).
* Permanent matter (natural rock, etc.) requires **Permanent** Duration to annihilate.
