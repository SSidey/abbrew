## **Preface**

This chapter explains the grammar of magic — how a spell is conceived, shaped, and sustained within the world.
It describes magic not as a list of discrete abilities, but as a language: every spell a sentence, every word an act of will.
Players can use this guide to build and cast their own workings, balancing the quick flow of thought (Aether) against the slow weight of the world (Gravitas).
Those who master these principles are not merely spellcasters, but speakers of the world’s oldest tongue.

---

## **The Nature of Magic (Aether and Gravitas)**

---

*“Aether is thought in motion;
Gravitas is the world’s reply.”*

---

Magic flows through two inseparable forces:

| **Aspect**   | **Meaning**                                                                                        | **Measure** |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| **Aether**   | The realm of will, imagination, and instant creation. Governs how quickly a spell can be shaped.   | **Actions** |
| **Gravitas** | The realm of resistance: time, matter, and duration. Governs how long and how far a spell endures. | **Entropy** |

### **Aether**

Aetheric power is volatile and immediate. Each component of a spell costs *actions* to imagine and direct.
Most combat magic, invocations, and short miracles occur wholly in the Aetheric sphere.

### **Gravitas**

Gravitic power is patient but heavy.
It demands cost — resolve, materials, or time.
Its measure is *entropy*, a count of the world’s inertia.
To wield Gravitas is to anchor imagination so reality accepts it.

**Instant magic** (Aether) consumes *actions*.
**Lingering magic** (Gravitas) consumes *entropy*.
Every working balances the two.

## **Casting a Spell (Ars Voluntas)**

---

*“A spell is a sentence written upon the air;
each word a promise the world must keep.”*

---

### **The Grammar of Casting**

All spells follow the same pattern:

> **Intent → Concept → Delivery → (Area) → (Duration)**

Each part adds either **actions** (Aetheric cost) or **entropy** (Gravitic cost).
Even a trained mage’s swift casting carries a latent gravitic weight — those without study may still perform the same act through ritual, paying its *entropy* instead of *actions*.

| **Component** | **Base Cost**                                              | **Description**                                                     |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Intent**    | 1 Action or 1 Entropy                                      | What you wish to do — the verb of magic.                            |
| **Concept**   | 1 Action or 1 Entropy (once known)                         | The substance or idea acted upon — *Fire, Flesh, Light, Fear*, etc. |
| **Delivery**  | Self 1 a · Touch 2 a · Near 3 a · Far 1 round · Remote 5 e | The range or target of the spell.                                   |
| **Area**      | +1 Entropy per step beyond a point                         | The shape or scale of effect (Line, Cone, Burst).                   |
| **Duration**  | Variable (see Ars Tempus)                                  | How long the effect persists.                                       |

A straightforward *instantaneous* spell — **Intent + Concept + Delivery** — typically costs **3 to 5 actions**, keeping most castings within a single combat round.

---

| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |

Each intent forms the **verb** of a spell.
A caster’s study in an intent allows them to pay its Aetheric cost in actions; those without study may still perform it through ritual, paying its Gravitic cost in entropy instead.

---

### **Action and Entropy Summary**

* A spellcaster has **five actions per round**.
* Each component always bears an **entropy value**; study converts that entropy to **actions** instead.
* Gravitic components (durations, areas, rituals) are paid wholly in **entropy**.
* Entropy may be offset by **Resolve**, **Focus**, or **Ritual time**.
* When all costs are met, the spell manifests.

> *“Those who speak swiftly shape briefly;
> those who ponder deeply bind the world for longer.”*

# **Part II – The Essence of the World (Ars Essentia)**

---

*“The world is made of meanings.
To name them is to touch the first language.”*

---

## **Understanding Ars Essentia**

Ars Essentia governs the *concepts* of magic — the **nouns** in a spell’s grammar.
Each represents a class of reality, from simple matter to the most abstract forces.
Magicians divide these into **Circuli** (Circles of Understanding), each further broken into **Orbis** (Spheres of Refinement).

The deeper the Circulus, the more abstract the Essentia — and the greater its Entropy.
A novice may shape clay and water; a master contends with death, faith, and void.

---

### **The Six Circuli of Essentia**

Entropy costs follow the ascending pattern **1–15**, three steps per Circulus.
Each Circulus contains three Orbis: **Minor**, **Median**, and **Major**.
Examples are suggestive, not exhaustive; the GM may assign nearby concepts to the closest Orbis when players innovate.

---

## **Circulus Null – Nihil (Nothingness)**

---

*“From silence all words are born.”*

**Entropy:** 0
**Nature:** The conceptual void; absence, negation, the state before being.
**Behaviour:** Nihil is not destruction but potential — the mirror opposite of creation.
It exists conceptually as a zero-point, used in transmutation to determine the *difference* between two Essentia.

* **Orbis Absolutus:** Pure absence, void, nullity.
* **Uses:** Required for *Create*, *Destroy*, and *Transmute*; forms the baseline for comparing all Circuli.

---

## **Circulus I – Material (Materia)**

---

*“Matter remembers touch and takes shape again.”*

**Entropy:** 1–3
**Nature:** The tangible world: physical materials and organic life.
**Behaviour:** Material Essentia responds readily to will. Its solidity grants reliability and resistance; spells acting on it are most common among artisans, druids, and fleshcrafters.

* **Orbis Minor (1e):** Stone, wood, soil, cloth, water.

  * *Simple, inert matter.*
* **Orbis Median (2e):** Flesh, blood, bone, hair.

  * *Living or once-living matter; mutable and warm.*
* **Orbis Major (3e):** Metal, crystal, construct, refined material.

  * *Resilient, deliberate structures; forged or grown by intent.*

---

## **Circulus II – Elemental (Elementa)**

---

*“The passions of the world burn, flow, breathe, and break.”*

**Entropy:** 4–6
**Nature:** Dynamic states of matter — flame, frost, storm, current.
**Behaviour:** Elemental Essentia are volatile, responding strongly to emotion and movement.
Mages of this Circulus shape nature’s fury and grace.

* **Orbis Minor (4e):** Fire, water, air, earth.

  * *The four classical elements, raw and immediate.*
* **Orbis Median (5e):** Lightning, magma, mist, acid, metal vapour.

  * *Compound or reactive elements; transitional forms.*
* **Orbis Major (6e):** Aether, shadow, vacuum, pure energy.

  * *Primordial essences beyond simple material states.*

---

## **Circulus III – Emotional (Affectus)**

---

*“Hearts burn hotter than any fire.”*

**Entropy:** 7–9
**Nature:** Passions, instincts, and psychic force.
**Behaviour:** Emotional Essentia links thought to effect; it shapes the unseen drives of creatures and crowds. Manipulation of Affectus is as dangerous to the caster as to the target.

* **Orbis Minor (7e):** Fear, rage, joy, sorrow.

  * *Individual emotions; immediate and contagious.*
* **Orbis Median (8e):** Courage, despair, lust, tranquility.

  * *Sustained moods; enduring emotional states.*
* **Orbis Major (9e):** Love, hate, faith, zeal.

  * *Binding passions; forces that move nations and gods.*

---

## **Circulus IV – Symbolic (Symbolum)**

---

*“The world dreams in signs; symbols remember what matter forgets.”*

**Entropy:** 10–12
**Nature:** Meaning, belief, and metaphysical identity.
**Behaviour:** Symbolic Essentia defines representation — words, runes, oaths, illusions, and cultural truths. Spells in this Circulus shape perception and narrative itself.

* **Orbis Minor (10e):** Light, shadow, sound, image.

  * *Surface symbols; sensory metaphors.*
* **Orbis Median (11e):** Name, language, sigil, story, voluntas.

  * *Cognitive symbols; language and lore. Also includes reference to Voluntas as a concept*
* **Orbis Major (12e):** Virtue, sin, destiny, memory.

  * *Archetypal symbols; moral and spiritual ideas.*

---

## **Circulus V – Cosmic (Cosmica)**

---

*“Stars remember the song before the world began.”*

**Entropy:** 13–15
**Nature:** Universal forces, divine principles, and abstract laws.
**Behaviour:** Cosmic Essentia governs creation’s architecture — life and death, entropy and order, fate and time. Few mortals grasp even its minor orbis safely.

* **Orbis Minor (13e):** Life, death, birth, decay.

  * *Natural cycles and metaphysical thresholds.*
* **Orbis Median (14e):** War, famine, pestilence, conquest.

  * *Planetary influences; mythic archetypes.*
* **Orbis Major (15e):** Sin, virtue, balance, void.

  * *Eternal principles; the self-aware laws of creation.*

---

### **Using Ars Essentia in Play**

Each spell draws upon **one or more Essentia**.

* The *entropy* of an Essentia determines its **gravitic weight**.
* Mastery of a Circulus allows a caster to treat that Essentia’s entropy as **actions** instead.
* Reusing the same concept within a single working costs no additional entropy once mastered (per *Lex Reductio Conceptus Perfecta*).

Casters may combine multiple Essentia to form *compound effects* — e.g., *Fire + Venin (Purity)* to create cleansing flame — paying the entropy of both or the higher when one modifies the other.

> *“To name a thing truly is to own its reflection.”*

## **Ars Trajecta – The Art of Space**

Ars Spatium defines **where** a spell takes effect, its cost is aetheric.

---

### **Delivery and Range**

| **Form of Delivery**      | **Description**                                                 | **Aetheric Cost** | **Entropy Cost** |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------- |
| **Self (Se)**             | The caster’s own body or aura.                                  | 1 a               | 0 e              |
| **Touch (Tactus)**        | Direct physical contact (≈ 1 m).                                | 2 a               | 1 e              |
| **Near (Proximum)**       | A few paces (≈ 10 m).                                           | 3 a               | 2 e              |
| **Far (Longinquum)**      | Visible range (≈ 100 m).                                        | 1 round (5 a)     | 3 e              |
| **Remote (Remotum)**      | Beyond sight (≈ 1 km); requires arcane link or symbolic anchor. | —                 | 5 e              |
| **Country (Provincia)**   | Regional scale (≈ 100 km).                                      | —                 | 6 e              |
| **Continent (Continens)** | Continental scale (≈ 1 000 km).                                 | —                 | 7 e              |
| **Planet (Mundus)**       | Planet-wide reach.                                              | —                 | 8 e              |
| **Planar (Planaris)**     | Across planes or realities.                                     | —                 | 9 e              |

> *Distance resists will: each step outward demands time, focus, or faith.*

---

## **Ars Forma - The art of Area and Shape**

Ars Forma defines the shape of an effect, its cost is gravitic and combines with Ars Tempus.

| **Shape**          | **Base Scale (Entropy 1)** | **Entropy Increase per Step**   | **Notes**                     |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **Point**          | Single target / object     | 0 e                             | Default.                      |
| **Line**           | 10 m × 1 m                 | +1 e per doubling (20 m, 40 m…) | +1 e to convert into a wall.  |
| **Cone**           | 5 m × 3 m at base          | +1 e per doubling (10 m, 20 m…) | Expands from caster or point. |
| **Burst / Sphere** | 2 m radius                 | +1 e per doubling (4 m, 8 m…)   | Detonates or emanates.        |
| **Emanation**      | Caster-centred aura        | +1 e per doubling radius        | Moves with caster.            |

> *To fill space with will is to argue with gravity itself.*

## **Ars Tempus – The Art of Time**

No effect exists without cost in time.
Every non-instantaneous spell carries *entropy*; each point demands **ritual** to bind imagination within the world’s rhythm.
A spell of 0 entropy requires no ritual; all others must be anchored by rite, focus, or expenditure of Resolve.

---

| **Entropy** | **Sustained Duration** | **Periodic Interval (Recurs Every …)** | **Ritual Time Required**    |
| ----------: | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------: |
|           0 | Instant (1 action)     | —                                      |                        —    |
|           1 | 1 Round (≈ 5 actions)  | 10 Years                               |                 1 action    | 
|           2 | 1 Minute               | 1 Year                                 |                  1 Round    |
|           3 | 10 Minutes             | 1 Month                                |                 1 Minute    |
|           4 | 1 Hour                 | 1 Week                                 |               10 Minutes    |

|           7 | 1 Week                 | 1 Hour                                 |                    1 Day    |
|           8 | 1 Month                | 10 Minutes                             |                   1 Week    |
|           9 | 1 Year                 | 1 Minute                               |                  1 Month    | 
|          10 | 10 Years               | -                                      |                   1 Year    |
|          11 | 100 Years              | -                                      |                   10 Years  |
|          12 | Permanent              | 1 Round                                |                   100 Years |

*The left column shows sustained durations; the middle shows equivalent entropy for periodic recurrences. Higher entropy yields shorter intervals.*

---

### **Manipulating Time in Practice**

* **Resolve Compression:** Spend Resolve equal to the entropy difference between your ritual’s length and desired compression (e.g., 3 Resolve to shorten a 10-minute ritual to 1 minute).
* **Implements:** Foci, loci, or anchors reduce effective entropy by their capacity, representing physical stabilisation of the working.
* **Study of Chronurgy:** Each rank reduces required ritual time one band (1 Week → 1 Day, etc.).

> *“To stretch a second into a century is easy—
> the price is simply all your tomorrows.”*

---

### **Combining Space and Time**

When a spell occupies both distance and duration, add their entropy values to determine the **total Gravitas**.
Any positive total enforces a ritual unless mitigated by Ars Materia or Ars Lex.
This total also determines the baseline for crafting or anchoring the spell into objects or sites.

> *“A moment’s flame may be born in breath;
> a wall of fire asks the patience of hours.”*

## Ars Conjunctio — The Grammar of Magical Syntax

> *“All thought seeks coherence; all power seeks a path.”*
> — The Grammar of Force, Vol. II

Magic is not a single word but a sentence.
Every additional clause adds meaning — and entropy.
**Ars Conjunctio** governs the structure of these clauses,
defining when an effect extends, repeats, reacts, or endures.

Each connective (“Conjunctive Clause”) adds **+1 Entropy** to the total cost of the spell,
representing the strain of maintaining coherence between separate intentions.

| **Conjunctive Word** | **Common Translation** | **Purpose / Logical Role**                     | **Entropy Added** | **Example Fragment**       |
| -------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------- |
| **et**               | *and*                  | Links equal effects simultaneously             | +1 e              | *Create Fire and Light*    |
| **vel**              | *or*                   | Allows one of several outcomes                 | +1 e              | *Heal or Shield*           |
| **quod**             | *that*                 | Introduces a subordinate or descriptive clause | +1 e              | *Create Flame that Burns*  |
| **cum**              | *when*                 | Introduces a trigger condition                 | +1 e              | *Ignite when Life is Near* |
| **donec**            | *until*                | Sets a condition for persistence or cessation  | +1 e              | *Endure until Dawn*        |

> **Rule of Simplicity:**
> Every *Conjunctio* word adds **exactly +1 Entropy** regardless of the number of linked clauses.
> All subsequent effects must still pay their own internal costs.
> A spell of three connected clauses therefore adds **+3 Entropy** for its structure,
> in addition to each clause’s individual Entropy.

**Example – Triggered Sigil**

> *Creare Sigillum ad Tactum; quod Creare Ignem Hic in Flatu; cum Vita Detecta ad Tactum.*
> “Create a sigil at touch; that creates fire here in a burst; when life is detected at touch.”

Breakdown:

* *Creare Sigillum ad Tactum* → 13 e
* *quod Creare Ignem Hic in Flatu* → 7 e
* *cum Vita Detecta ad Tactum* → 16 e
* +3 e total for the conjunctions
  **Final Entropy:** 39 e before reductions.

## **I. Building a Spell**

Magic follows a simple order:
**Intent → Essentia → Delivery (+ Duration or Area if desired).**
Each element adds entropy unless internalised or offset by foci, loci, or ritual.

---

### **Example 1 – Firebolt**

**Creare Ignis Proximum** — “Create fire near.”

| Component    | Meaning                                            | Cost (Untrained) | Cost (Trained) |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------- |
| **Creare**   | To create, calling matter or energy from potential | 1 e              | 1 a            |
| **Ignis**    | Fire, heat, flame                                  | 5 e              | 1 a            |
| **Proximum** | Near—within a few metres                           | 3 e              | 3 a            |

→ **9 entropy** for the uninitiated, or **5 actions** for a caster who has mastered all three.
This forms a dart or burst of flame: *Firebolt.*

---

### **Example 2 – The Flame That Spreads**

**Creare Ignis Proximum per Unum Minutum; qui Creare Tactu Singulis Rotis.**
*“Create fire near for one minute; that creates itself by touch each round.”*

The semicolon marks a **sub-clause**—a secondary action nested within the first.

| Element               | Effect                                             | Added Entropy |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| **Creare (repeated)** | *Reflected Intent* — same action within same spell | –1 e          |
| **Tactu**             | Touch range for spread                             | 2 e           |
| **Each Round**        | Periodic effect (inverse duration)                 | 11 e          |

≈ **19 extra entropy** beyond the base 9.
Such a working demands foci, loci, or ritual preparation.

> *“Some flames learn to walk.”*

| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Intelligence**  | Determines what Essentia can be learned or internalised.                                | Tier + Intelligence Score  | May learn up to Circulus III Orbis I (7 e concepts).            |

| **Visualisation** | Reduces gravitic entropy through Foci. Simple = ×1 Vis; Aligned <= ×2; Perfected <= ×3. | Visualisation Rank         | Simple focus reduces up to 9 entropy.                           |
| **Wits**          | Limits number of components in a spell.                                                 | Wits Rank                  | May combine 9 components;                                       |
|                   | Power of interdiction                                                                   | Tier + Wits Score          | Interdiction with 10 entropy.                                   |
| **Resolve**       | Absorbs backlash and fatigue from failure or strain.                                    |                            | —                                                               |

## **III. Overextension and Containment**

When a spell’s entropy exceeds your Will, it becomes unstable.

### **Stabilisation Attempt**

To bend excess power without shattering:

* Roll **1d10 + Will** against **uncontrolled entropy** *(Entropy − Will)*.
* Each attempt consumes **one Tier Die**—a limited daily resource.

* A Tier 1 caster has 1 Tier Die, Tier 3 has 3, and so on.
* You cannot attempt stabilisation once all Tier Dice are spent.

| Result                          | Outcome                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Success**                     | Spell manifests. You lose Resolve equal to the higher of **half the uncontrolled entropy** (rounded up) or **their will score** and cannot cast next round.                     |
| **Failure**                     | Spell collapses. Lose Resolve equal to the higher of **the full uncontrolled entropy.** or **their will score**                                                                 |
| **Critical Failure (1 on die)** | Backlash: the magic escapes control. Resolve drops to 0 and you suffer an effect matching the spell’s nature (fire burns, time stutters, mind fractures). Exhausted until rest. |

You may not attempt to stabilise any spell whose entropy is more than **twice your Will**—the effort simply fails.

> *“Power obeys courage, but not mercy.”*

---

### **Example – Risking Overreach**

Our caster (Will 7) attempts the 19-entropy *Flame That Spreads.*

* Safe limit = 7 e
* Excess = 12 e

They roll **1d10 + 7 vs 12**.

* On Success: spell holds; Resolve – 6.
* On Failure: spell collapses; Resolve – 12.
* On 1: backlash, Resolve = 0, surroundings ignite.

---

### **Instability and Tradition**

* Each further point of excess entropy after failure increases the severity of backlash.
* Some magical traditions may later gain talents to convert limited overreach into controlled effects, spending Resolve intentionally.
* Such paths are rare; most sages warn that “the third overreach is always fatal.”

## Ars Materia — Of Implements, Anchors, and Loci

> *“Matter remembers the shape of thought.”*
> — Treatise on Resonant Craft

### **Purpose**

**Ars Materia** concerns the physical tools that bind, focus, or sustain magical workings:
the *foci* that stabilise Aetheric entropy, and the *anchors* and *loci* that contain Gravitic power.

---

### **1. Foci (Focus, plural Foci)**

A **Focus** is any material object that symbolically or materially resonates with the spell’s Essentia.
The most mundane focus — a torch for *Ignis*, a vial of blood for *Sanguis* — counts as a **simple** focus (×1 band).
Foci lessen Gravitic Entropy, drawing upon the caster’s **Visualisation** to do so.

#### **Focus Grades**

| **Grade**     | **Reduction Limit** | **Cost to Refine (+1 Band)** | **Notes**                                                 |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Simple**    | ≤ Visualisation     | Free / mundane               | Natural or symbolic materials (e.g. flame, stone, water). |
| **Aligned**   | ≤ 2 × Visualisation | + 300 gp per band            | Crafted or consecrated for its Essentia.                  |
| **Perfected** | ≤ 3 × Visualisation | + 300 gp × Circulus          | Rare, often unique, masterworks.                          |

Each refinement stage grants a +1 band improvement within that type’s limit.
Foci use requires training in the relevant craft or art (e.g. **Artificing, Enchanting, or Consecration**).

# **Part V – Ars Materia**

### *Of Foci, Loci, and Anchors*

> *“Where thought touches matter, the world remembers.”*
> — *The Codex of Binding*, Vol. I

---

## **1 · On Foci**

A *focus* is an enduring alignment between physical matter and an Essentia.
It acts as a conduit to reduce entropy when the spell shares its aligned concept.
A torch for *Ignis*, a mirror for *Lux*, a sprig of oak for *Vita*—each channels order through likeness.

To **create or enhance** a focus:

> *aligere [essentia] hic pro [duration]*
> *e.g.* *aligere ignis hic pro aeternam* — Align Fire Here for Eternity.
>
> Base entropy: 1e (intent) + 5e (essentia) + 2e (location) + 12e (duration) = **20e total**.
>
> Cost: 300 gp × total e = 6 000 gp.
>
> Each enhancement increases capacity by +5e for that fire.
>
> Aligned foci (x2) and perfected foci (x3) may be created with greater investments or with specific material correspondences.

Use of refined foci requires training in Artificing, Enchanting, or related crafts.

| **Grade**   | **Reduction Limit** | **Refinement (+1 Band)** | **Description**                              |
| ----------- | ------------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| *Simple*    | ≤ Visualisation     | — (mundane)              | Naturally resonant object or circumstance.   |
| *Aligned*   | ≤ 2 × Visualisation | + 300 gp per band        | Crafted or consecrated for its Essentia.     |
| *Perfected* | ≤ 3 × Visualisation | + 300 gp × Circulus      | Singular masterwork, often unique or sacred. |

Typically a focus must change form when becoming more aligned to a concept 
> e.g. a ruby ring might suffice for a simple fire focus
> When it becomes aligned it would be incorporated into something that fits in one hand e.g. embedded in a torch or used as a band around the torch.
> When it becomes perfected it would then be incorporated into something like a staff, a two handed item
> Further still and the line between focus and locus may blend as it could then be incorporated into a pyre, an immovable focus or locus.

> *“A tool does not make a mage—but the world prefers a handle.”*

---

## **2 · On Loci**

A **Locus** is a permanent, sigilised space—an immovable focus—that anchors entropy to earth and stone.
It draws instability away within its bounds, marks ritual sites, or serves as a coordinate for transit and recall.

Creating a locus is a ritual act using the standard syntax of composition.

> *Creare Sigillum Hic per Unum Diem in Circulo Unum Metrum; quod Alligare ad Ignem.*
> “Create a sigil here for one day in a circle one metre wide; that binds to fire.”

**Entropy breakdown**
Creare (1 e) + Sigillum (11 e) + Hic (0 e) + Tempus 1 Diem (6 e) + Forma Circuli (1 e) + Conjunctio (1 e) + Alligare ad Ignem (5 e) = **25 e**

Such a locus persists for one day, offsetting up to a caster's visualisation rank in entropy of spells containing *Ignis* within its circle.
If inscribed instead to a symbolic mark (*“that binds to this sigil”*), it functions as a fixed coordinate for ritual linkages such as teleportation or divination.

> *“To mark a place is to teach the world your language.”*

---

## **3 · On Anchors**

An **Anchor** binds a spell’s structure into physical form — engraved steel, inked parchment, set gemstone.
It is the vessel of Aetheric memory, holding not thought but pattern.

* **Capacity** = Entropy the anchor can hold (any additional must be supplied to recharge the effect).
* **Remainder** = Entropy to be supplied each time the item is recharged.
* **Material Cost** ≈ 300 gp × capacity in e.

> *Example — Ring of Fire:*
> Spell = *Creare Ignem Proximum* (9 e).
> Anchor capacity = 5 e.
> We would need to follow Imbuere (Creare Ignem Proximum; when detect command word) at touch permanently
> 1ge (1ge 5ge 3ge; 1ge 1ge 11ge)* 1ge 12ge for a total cost of 36ge. 
> Supplying a fire focus and using a locus of creation e.g. simple torch and an enchanted anvil would allow the crafter to reduce the cost by e.g. 14ge if they have 7 ranks in Visualisation.
> This would bring us to 10ae and 12ge, with further applications of restrictions via Ars Lex and material investment we could reduce this further.

> *“The ring knows its hour; it sleeps until the world speaks its name.”*

### ⟢ Part VI ⟣

# **Ars Praxis – The Practice of Casting**

> *“All creation is the union of light and weight; thought rises, matter pulls, and magic is born between them.”*

---

## **I · Two Realms of Entropy**

Every working of magic flows between two poles of being.

| Realm                     | Components                                                         | Nature                      | How It’s Reduced                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Aetheric Entropy (ae)** | *Intent (Voluntas)* · *Concept (Essentia)* · *Delivery (Trajecta)* | Mutable, mental, inward     | Reduced through **study and internalisation**. 1 ae → 1 action (a).                                                                                    |
| **Gravitic Entropy (ge)** | *Form (Forma)* · *Duration (Tempus)* · *Conjunction (Conjunctio)*  | Resistant, worldly, outward | Reduced only through **external aids** — foci, loci, sacrifice, resolve, or Lex.  ge costs are **cumulative** (7 ge = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 = 28). |

> *“The mind shapes the spark; the world decides how long it burns.”*

---

## **II · The Shape of a Spell**

A spell is a sentence spoken in the grammar of the Ars:

**Intent + Concept + Delivery (+ Form + Duration)** ; (+ Conjunction + another spell)

Each element contributes entropy.
Aetheric parts can be learned and acted upon; Gravitic parts must be bargained down.

---

## **III · Means of Reduction**

| Source                      | Ars                            | Function                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Internalised Components** | *Voluntas, Essentia, Trajecta* | Convert ae → a.                                                                                  |
| **Foci**                    | *Materia*                      | Reduce total entropy by up to **Visualisation × grade** (Simple ×1 · Aligned ×2 · Perfected ×3). |
| **Loci**                    | *Materia*                      | Stationary foci reducing entropy by their capacity.                                              |
| **Lex Constraints**         | *Lex*                          | Add limits, costs, or conditions to offset 1 e each.                                             |
| **Resolve**                 | —                              | May substitute 1 e per point; or spent to stabilise excess ge.                                   |

---

## **IV · Example I – Creare Ignem Proximum**

Plain English: *Create fire near.*

| Component | Ars      | Realm    | Cost | Result       |
| --------- | -------- | -------- | ---- | ------------ |
| Creare    | Voluntas | Aetheric | 1 ae | 1 a if known |
| Ignem     | Essentia | Aetheric | 5 ae | 1 a if known |
| Proximum  | Trajecta | Aetheric | 3 ae | 1 a if known |

**Total:** 9 ae.

* **Untrained:** 9 ae ≈ 1-year ritual.
* **Trained:** All components internalised → 5 actions (≈ 1 round).
* **With Focus:** A simple torch (Vis 7) offsets up to 7 ae when used in ritual, or stabilises minor overreach in open casting.
* **Within a Locus of Fire (5 ae capacity):** same spell may be worked by a novice in ≈ 10 minutes.

> *“The simplest flame teaches all that is needful.”*

## **V · Example II – Creare Sigillum Hic per Unum Diem in Circulo Unum Metrum ; quod Alligare ad Ignem**

Plain English: *Create a sigil here for one day in a one-metre circle; that binds to fire.*

| Component           | Ars        | Realm    | Base  | Reduction                | Notes                        |
| ------------------- | ---------- | -------- | ----- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Creare              | Voluntas   | Aetheric | 1 ae  | 1 a                      | —                            |
| Sigillum            | Essentia   | Aetheric | 11 ae | 1 a                      | —                            |
| Hic                 | Trajecta   | Aetheric | 0 ae  | 1 a                      | —                            |
| Alligare            | Voluntas   | Aetheric | 1 ae  | 1 a                      | —                            |
| Ignem               | Essentia   | Aetheric | 5 ae  | 1 a                      | —                            |
| Forma (Circle 1 m)  | Forma      | Gravitic | 0 ge  | —                        | —                            |
| Tempus (1 day)      | Tempus     | Gravitic | 6 ge  | -                        | 7 ge cumulative (28 total e) |
| Conjunctio (“that”) | Conjunctio | Gravitic | 1 ge  | -                        | —                            |

**Aetheric:** 5 actions.
**Gravitic:** 7 ge (≈ 1-week duration requiring a 1 day ritual).

**Reductions:**

* Simple Fire Focus (Vis 7) → −7 ge → 6 ge.
* Material investment ≈ 6 × 300 gp in brass or fire-aligned mediums, or 6 Resolve / Vital Wounds / Lex clauses to offset would bring us to 5ge for the equivalent of a 12 hour duration or 1 hour ritual

**Lex Options:**

* *Verbum* (incantation) −1 e  · *Personal attunement* −1 e  · *Sacrificium sanguinis* −1 e per Vital Wound  · *Pactum* −1 e per Resolve borrowed  · *Vinculum* (only within circle) −1 e.

> *“Each mark remembers the hand that drew it; each fire, the breath that named it.”*

## **Ars Lex – On the Binding of Law and Limit**

> *“Where power flows unbounded, ruin follows.
> Thus we write the Law, that the world may remain.”*

**Ars Lex** concerns the deliberate imposition of boundaries upon the workings of aether and gravitas.
Through *Lex*, the magus declares a law — a binding word that defines when, where, or by whom a spell may be wrought.
Each restriction tempers chaos, transforming wild potential into stable form.
Entropy once free becomes *obedient* to rule, and in that obedience the spell is refined.

---

### **I. Nature of Lex**

Every *Lex* reduces the total **entropy** (*e*) of a spell after all aetheric and gravitic calculations have been made.
It represents the *Law of Limitation* — a principle that curtails use, scope, or circumstance.

> **Ae + Ge − Le = Total Entropy**

Where:

* **Ae** = Aetheric Entropy (thought, intent, internal complexity)
* **Ge** = Gravitic Entropy (form, duration, external action)
* **Le** = Lex Entropy (reduction from limitation or sacrifice)

A Lex may never reduce total entropy below **1 e**.
Multiple Lex may apply, though each successive imposition is subject to diminishing return — the stronger the Law, the less room remains for further constraint.

---

### **II. Forms of Lex**

#### **1. Lex Tempus — Law of Time**

A limitation of frequency or recurrence.

| **Condition**        | **Reduction (Le)** |
| :---------------     | :----------------: |
| Once per round       |        − 1 e       |
| Once per minute      |        − 2 e       |
| Once per 10 minutes  |        − 3 e       |
| Once per hour        |        − 4 e       |
| Once per hour        |        − 5 e       |
| Once per day         |        − 6 e       |
| Once per week        |        − 7 e       |
| Once per month       |        − 8 e       |
| Once per year        |        − 9 e       |
| Once per decade      |        − 10 e      |
| Once per century     |        − 11 e      |
| Once, ever           |        − 12 e      |

*Example:*

> *Lex Tempus: semel in noctem solum* — the spell may be cast only once each night.
> Reduction = − 3 e.

---

#### **2. Lex Repletio — Law of Recharge**

A limitation of automatic recharge.

| Recharge Interval | Reduction |
| ----------------- | --------- |
| **1 round**       | **1e**    |
| **1 minute**      | **2e**    |
| **10 minutes**    | **3e**    |
| **1 hour**        | **4e**    |
| **12 hours**      | **5e**    |
| **1 day**         | **6e**    |
| **1 week**        | **7e**    |
| **1 month**       | **8e**    |
| **1 year**        | **9e**    |
| **10 years**      | **10e**   |
| **100 years**     | **11e**   |

---

#### **3. Lex Restitutio — Law of Manual Recharge**

A limitation of manual recharge. 
The bearer must spend the required duration in form a ritual to recharge the enchantment on an anchor.

| Recharge Interval | Reduction |
| ----------------- | --------- |
| **1 round**       | **1e**    |
| **1 minute**      | **2e**    |
| **10 minutes**    | **3e**    |
| **1 hour**        | **4e**    |
| **12 hours**      | **5e**    |
| **1 day**         | **6e**    |
| **1 week**        | **7e**    |
| **1 month**       | **8e**    |
| **1 year**        | **9e**    |
| **10 years**      | **10e**   |
| **100 years**     | **11e**   |

---

#### **4. Lex Conditionis — Law of Circumstance**

A limitation of state or situation.

Each unique condition imposes a reduction proportional to rarity or specificity:

| **Example**                           | **Typical Reduction** |
| :------------------------------------ | :-------------------: |
| Only at night                         |         − 2 e         |
| Only beneath a full moon              |         − 3 e         |
| Only when blood is spilled            |         − 4 e         |
| Only within sanctified ground         |         − 5 e         |
| Only when the magus bleeds or suffers |         − 6 e         |

Multiple conditions may combine, though reductions do not exceed − 10 e in total.

---

#### **5. Lex Persona — Law of Bearer**

A limitation of identity or inheritance.

| **Restriction**                              | **Reduction (Le)** |
| :------------------------------------------- | :----------------: |
| Only for the caster                          |        − 2 e       |
| Only for one named individual                |        − 3 e       |
| Bound to a line of blood or succession       |        − 5 e       |
| Bound to no mortal hand (save by ritual key) |        − 7 e       |

Such bindings form the basis of inheritance spells, blood-oaths, and relics that awaken only in rightful hands.

---

#### **6. Lex Sacrificium — Law of Offering**

A limitation through *price* — where entropy is paid in blood, pain, or resolve.

| **Offering**                          |     **Reduction (Le)**    |
| :------------------------------------ | :-----------------------: |
| Expenditure of Resolve (−1 per point) |         − 1 e each        |
| Infliction of Vital Wound             |      − 1 e per wound      |
| Loss of Memory or Virtue              | − 3 e per aspect forsaken |

Each sacrifice is permanent until restored through greater working.
The Law remembers.

---

#### **7. Lex Vinculum — Law of Bond**

A limitation bound by *oath* or *curse*.
When broken, the Law exacts its price in suffering, often manifesting as immediate corruption, madness, or dissolution of the bound magic.

> *Lex Vinculum: si rumpitur, tunc magus comburet.*
> “If the Law is broken, the magus shall burn.”
>
> Reduction = − 5 e (conditional), increased to − 8 e if the penalty is fatal.

---

### **III. Application**

A spell may contain multiple Lex, each declared at the time of composition or inscription.
Each Law is written as a clause following the principal syntax, prefixed by *Lex ...*.
For example:

> *creare ignis proximum — Lex Tempus semel in die; Lex Sacrificium unus vulnus magus.*
> “Create Fire Nearby — only once per day; the magus must suffer one wound.”
>
> Base Entropy: 8 e
> Le = (− 4 e − 2 e) = − 6 e
> Final Entropy = 2 e.

## **✦ Imbuere: The Law of Material Permanence**

> *“What endures in the flesh of the world endures in thought.
> To inscribe is to command the stone to remember.”* — *De Lex et Materia, Vol. III*

### **Definition**

**Imbuere** (to suffuse, to infuse) is the art of binding aetheric form into enduring matter.
Through this act the permanence of a spell ceases to rely upon gravitic sustainment, instead being upheld by the inherent constancy of the medium that bears it.
Thus, *matter itself becomes the vessel of endurance,* and the cost of perpetuity is transmuted from entropy to substance.

---

### **Principle**

A spell bound by *Imbuere* substitutes the **gravitic cost of permanence** with the **durability of its host matter**.
This act establishes a *synthetic permanence*—an equilibrium wherein the material’s endurance satisfies the gravitic demand of the enchantment.

| Medium Type                                                 |         Treated Duration        | Gravitic Cost | Notes                                              |
| :---------------------------------------------------------- | :-----------------------------: | :-----------: | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ephemeral** (wax, parchment, cloth)                       |         1 day to 1 week         |     +3 ge     | subject to decay; temporary enchantments           |
| **Durable** (stone, bronze, steel, bone)                    |          **Permanent**          |      0 ge     | sustains enchantment so long as the medium endures |
| **Immutable / Mythic** (adamant, relic bone, locus crystal) | **Permanent + Repletionis + 1** |      0 ge     | capable of natural recharge or slow regeneration   |

---

### **Ritual Structure**

The syntax of an Imbuere enchantment follows that of a spell, with *Imbuere* as the governing Intent:

```
Imbuere (Creare Ignem Proximum; quando revelare verbum) per tactum; pro aeternam
```

Here, *Imbuere* defines the act of infusion, *Creare Ignem Proximum* the internalised spell to be held within, and the subsequent clauses (*quando revelare verbum*, *per tactum*) describe the manner and condition of manifestation.

**Example:**
A torch that conjures flame when the word “Flamma” is spoken:

```
Imbuere (Creare Ignem Proximum; quando revelare verbum "Flamma") per tactum
```

| Component       | Type        |            Entropy            | Notes                            |
| :-------------- | :---------- | :--------------------------:  | :------------------------------- |
| Imbuere         | Intent      |             1 ae              | defines enchantment act          |
| Creare          | Intent      |             1 ae              | internalised spell origin        |
| Ignem           | Essentia    |             5 ae              | reduced by aligned focus (torch) |
| Proximum        | Delivery    |             2 ae              | internalised within item         |
| Revelare verbum | Conjunction |             12 ge             | activation clause                |
| **Total**       |             | **21 e → 16 e (after focus)** | permanent by medium              |

The enchantment persists so long as the torch remains materially intact.
Destruction or corrosion of the vessel dispels the enchantment, for its permanence was the vessel’s own.

---

### **Crafting and Cost**

The *Imbuere* act demands not gravitic energy but *material equivalence*—the conversion of potential entropy into the effort of creation.

* **Material Parity:** substitute each *ge* avoided with approximately **300 gp × ge-level** in materials and craftsmanship.
* **Optional Lex Vinculum:** (“only when held,” “only when commanded”) may further reduce total entropy.

---

### **Rule of Material Permanence**

> **If the medium endures, the enchantment endures.**
> The permanence of matter may wholly substitute the gravitic cost of *Tempus* if the spell is properly inscribed, etched, or fused into a durable or immutable vessel.
>
> **Formula:**
> *Tempus pro aeternam* (12 ge) → **0 ge** when bound via *Imbuere* to a qualifying medium.
>
> The destruction of the vessel severs the enchantment.
> Preservation of the vessel preserves the spell.
