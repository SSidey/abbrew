# ⚝ **Codex Canonica Magicarum — v4.3**

> *“Will shapes essence, essence defines form, form commands time, matter binds law.”*

---

## I · Ars Voluntas — *Art of Will*

All magical expression begins with **intent** — the purpose of a spell.
Each intent belongs to a **family (Familia Voluntatis)** and carries a distinct energetic profile.

| **Family (Latin)**     | **Intents (Latin / English)**                                                        | **Entropy Base** | **Function / Example**                        |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Formativa Primus**   | *Creare* (Create), *Destruere* (Destroy)                                             | Concept + 3 e    | Manifestation or unmaking of concepts         |
| **Formativa Secundus** | *Formare* (Shape), *Configurare* (Mould)                                             | 2 e              | Alteration of an existing concept’s structure |
| **Kinetica**           | *Movere* (Move), *Sistere* (Stop)                                                    | 1 e              | Motion and redirection of matter or energy    |
| **Integrativa**        | *Infundere* (Infuse), *Extrahere* (Extract)                                          | 2 e              | Combining or separating concepts              |
| **Protectiva**         | *Protegere ab / cum* (Protect from / with)                                           | 2 e              | Wards and resistances                         |
| **Cognitiva**          | *Revelare* (Reveal), *Obfuscare* (Obscure)                                           | 2 e              | Knowledge, illusion, deception                |
| **Temporalis**         | *Morari* (Delay), *Sustinere* (Sustain), *Revertere* (Revert), *Promovere* (Advance) | 3 e              | Manipulation of persistence                   |
| **Destructiva**        | *Laedere* (Harm), *Sanare* (Heal)                                                    | 1 e              | Transference of vitality or damage            |
| **Transmutativa**      | *Transmutare* (Transform)                                                            | variable         | Alteration of one concept into another        |

### Transmutation Cost

Transmutation’s difficulty scales with conceptual distance.

| **Change Type**        | **Additional Cost**        |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Within same Orbis      | + 1 e                      |
| Between adjacent Orbes | + 1 e per step             |
| Between Circuli        | + 2 e per Circulus crossed |
| Base friction          | + 1 e                      |

> Example: *Flesh → Flame* (Corpus → Ignis, 1 Circulus)
> = Base 1 + Orbis 1 + Circulus 2 = **4 extra e**

## II · Ars Essentia — *Art of Essence*

Concepts are grouped into **five Circuli**, each with three **Orbes** (sub-circles) representing depth of understanding.

| **Circulus**  | **Theme** | **Orbis Minor**   | **Orbis Medius**   | **Orbis Maior**       | **Examples**         | **Base Entropy (e)** |
| ------------- | --------- | ----------------- | ------------------ | --------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Corpus**    | Material  | Stone, wood, clay | Flesh, bone, blood | Metal, crystal, alloy | steel blade (3e)     | 1 – 3                |
| **Ignis**     | Elemental | Air, mist         | Fire, light        | Lightning, magma      | torch flame (5e)     | 4 – 6                |
| **Vita**      | Vital     | Root, plant, sap  | Poison, disease    | Life, soul            | healing essence (8e) | 7 – 9                |
| **Anima**     | Emotional | Fear, joy         | Rage, sorrow       | Virtue, sin           | courage aura (11e)   | 10 – 12              |
| **Aetherion** | Cosmic    | Void, shadow      | Fate, entropy      | Creation, divinity    | holy spark (14e)     | 13 – 15              |

## V · Ars Materia — *Art of Substance*

| **Focus Type**            | **Relation to Concept** | **Entropy Reduction**  | **Action Conversion**      |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **Simple (Simplex)**      | Symbolic affinity       | Reduces to Orbis Minor | —                          |
| **Aligned (Alligatus)**   | Consecrated alignment   | 0 Entropy              | Actions = original Entropy |
| **Perfected (Perfectus)** | True relic              | 0 Entropy              | 0 Actions                  |

**Material Components:**
Consumables are part of *Ars Materia*, not Limiters.
If a material is consumed, reduce total Entropy by 1 e per component destroyed (once per spell).

## VI · Ars Lex — *Art of Law*

*(Governing Verbum, Gestus, and Vinculi)*

The Ars Lex defines how will and law interact: through *voice*, *motion*, and *restriction*.
These create stability but expose the caster to observation or counterplay.

### Lex Sub-Arts

| **Sub-Art** | **Focus**      | **Function**                       |
| ----------- | -------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **Verbum**  | Speech / sigil | Words define the pattern of law    |
| **Gestus**  | Motion / sign  | Movement enacts the law            |
| **Vinculi** | Limitation     | Constraint reduces cost or entropy |

| **Method**               | **Art**         | **Effect**         | **Counter Risk**        |
| ------------------------ | --------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------- |
| **Silent Working**       | —               | + 1 e instability  | None                    |
| **Verbal Chant**         | Verbum          | – 1 e instability  | Audible; interruptible  |
| **Somatic Gesture**      | Gestus          | – 1 e instability  | Restrained = failure    |
| **Structured Casting**   | Verbum + Gestus | – 2 e instability  | Highly visible          |
| **True Name Invocation** | Verbum          | + 1 Chronurgy Band | If reflected = feedback |

### Limiters (Vinculi)

Limiters impose narrative or physical constraints for reduced entropy.

| **Limiter**                              | **Effect** | **Risk / Cost**            |
| ---------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------- |
| Line of Effect Required                  | – 1 e      | Must travel uninterrupted  |
| Fixed Origin Point                       | – 1 e      | Bound to locus             |
| Consumed Catalyst (see Materia)**        | – 1 e      | Consumes a linked material |
| Vocalisation Required                    | – 1 e      | Can be silenced            |
| Two-Hand Gesture Required                | – 1 e      | Restricts mobility         |
| Environmental Dependence (e.g. sunlight) | – 1 e      | Fails if condition lost    |
| Caster Immobile During Cast              | – 2 e      | No movement allowed        |
| Requires Symbol / Circle                 | – 1 e      | Needs prepared area        |

> Multiple Limiters may stack to a max – 3 e.
> Limiters overlap with *Ars Materia* when physical items are required or destroyed.

## VII · Example — *Creare Ignem Proximum* (“Create Fire Near”)

**Components**

| Part                    | Art                       | Entropy                   | Description             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Creare (Create)**     | Voluntas Formativa Primus | Concept + 3 = 5 + 3 = 8 e | Manifestation of Fire   |
| **Fire (Ignis Medius)** | Essentia                  | 5 e                       | Elemental Concept       |
| **Near (Proximus)**     | Spatium                   | 3 e                       | 10 m range              |
| **Form: Point**         | Spatium                   | 0 e                       | Default shape           |
| **Duration: Instant**   | Tempus                    | 0 e                       | Immediate manifestation |
| **Total**               | —                         | **11 e**                  | Baseline Entropy        |

**Scenario Calculations**

| **Condition**                               | **Adjustment**       | **Total Entropy / Actions**        |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Base cast (no study / no focus)             | —                    | 11 e ≈ 100-year ritual             |
| Studied Voluntas, Essentia, Spatium         | Converts 11 e → 11 a | 11 Actions (≈ 2 rounds + 1 action) |
| Add Simple Fire Focus (torch flame)**       | Fire 5 → 4           | 10 a                               |
| Add Aligned Focus (consecrated gem)         | Fire 4 → 0           | 6 a                                |
| Add Limiters (Line of Effect, Vocalisation) | – 2 e                | 4 a                                |
| Add Verbum + Gestus (Structured Casting)    | – 2 e                | **2 Actions (≈ 1 round)**          |

> **Final:** A fully-trained caster with proper focus, visible casting, and line-of-effect limitation can manifest *Create Fire Near* in one round.
> Without these, it remains a long ritual spell.

## VIII · Summary

| **Aspect**           | **Key Rule**                                          |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **Six Arts**         | Will → Essence → Space → Time → Substance → Law       |
| **Entropy**          | Universal cost; study or focus reduce to actions      |
| **Foci**             | Cost = 300 gp × Concept Entropy; creation time scales |
| **Limiters (Lex)**   | Up to –3 e through constraints                        |
| **Verbum / Gestus**  | – 1 each instability, visible signatures              |
| **Transmutation**    | + 1 base + 1 per Orbis + 2 per Circulus               |
| **Resolve**          | Entropy – Tier = cost to sustain                      |
| **Spatium Sub-Arts** | Distantia (range), Figura (form), Motus (motion)      |
| **Materia vs Lex**   | Components consumed = Materia; constraints = Lex      |
